## What is a User Group?

A **User Group** organizes users, metrics, and products within an organization to streamline human evaluation workflows.

User Groups serve three key purposes:

1. **Assigning evaluators**: Group users who should review and annotate AI outputs for specific metrics.
2. **Scoping metrics**: Link metrics to groups so that only users in those groups see pending evaluations for those metrics.
3. **Linking products**: Link products to a group, or mark a group as linked to **all products** of the organization, current and future. A READER or HUMAN_EVALUATOR sees the union of the products that all its groups link. When one of its groups links all products, it sees every product, including a product that no other group links. A READER or HUMAN_EVALUATOR in no group sees no product. OWNER, ADMIN and MEMBER users see every product of the organization, whatever their groups.

A [Human Evaluation](/concepts/metric/evaluation-types#human-evaluation) metric is routed strictly: only users in a group linked to that metric see its pending evaluations in the **Human Evaluations** page of the dashboard. A READER or HUMAN_EVALUATOR also sees only the evaluations of the products their groups grant. A metric with no linked group reaches nobody, and its metric page shows a warning.

A new organization gets an empty **"all products"** group. Organizations created before this feature also got this group, filled with their READER and HUMAN_EVALUATOR users. More than one group can be linked to all products.

## Managing User Groups

Only an OWNER or ADMIN may create, edit, or delete a user group, or change its users or products. A MEMBER can read every group of the organization. A MEMBER can also link or unlink a group of the organization to a metric that the MEMBER may edit.

User groups can be managed in two places:

- **Dashboard**: Navigate to an organization's **Groups** tab to create, edit, and delete groups. From the user detail page, an OWNER or ADMIN can assign and unassign groups. From the metric detail page, any user who may edit the metric can assign and unassign groups.
- **SDK**: Use the `galtea.user_groups` service for programmatic management. See the [Human Evaluation tutorial](/sdk/tutorials/human-evaluation) for a step-by-step guide.

## User Group Properties

<ResponseField name="id" type="string">
  Unique identifier for the user group.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="organization_id" type="string">
  The organization this group belongs to.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="name" type="string" required>
  Name of the user group.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="type" type="string" required>
  Type of user group. Currently only `EVALUATOR` is supported.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="description" type="string" optional>
  Description of the group's purpose.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="user_ids" type="list[string]">
  IDs of users in this group.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="metric_ids" type="list[string]">
  IDs of metrics linked to this group.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="product_ids" type="list[string]">
  IDs of products linked to this group. The products a group links are the only products its READER and HUMAN_EVALUATOR users can see.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="all_products" type="boolean">
  When `true`, the group is linked to every product of the organization, current and future,
  regardless of `product_ids`. A READER or HUMAN_EVALUATOR in the group then sees every product.
</ResponseField>
