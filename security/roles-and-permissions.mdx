Galtea uses role-based access control. Every member of your organization has one role that decides what they can see and change. This page lists the four organization roles and the actions each one can perform. Actions cover the standard operations (Create, Read, Update, Delete) plus a soft delete (a logical delete that hides a record without removing it). Many permissions are conditional, shown as a short rule in the cell.

## Roles

- **OWNER**: Manages users and resources in their own organization. Can permanently delete only Sessions and User Groups; every other entity is soft delete at most. Only OWNER may create, edit, or delete a User Group, or change its users or products.
- **MEMBER**: Day-to-day access in their organization: view and contribute, with limited edits. Can permanently delete only Sessions; every other entity is soft delete at most. Reads every User Group of the organization. Cannot create, edit, or delete a User Group, or change its users or products. May link or unlink a group of the organization to a metric it may edit.
- **HUMAN_EVALUATOR**: Read-only on the products their user groups grant, plus may annotate AI metrics and submit human evaluations on them.
- **READER**: Read-only on the products their user groups grant. Cannot create, update, or delete anything.

<Note>
  Each cell shows what a role can do for that action. ✅ means allowed and ❌ means not allowed. A short rule (for example, "If product in own org") means the action is allowed only when that rule is true at the time, checked against your organization and the record's ownership.
</Note>

## Notes

- **Read-only roles**: READER and HUMAN_EVALUATOR read what a MEMBER reads, except that products and everything inside a product are limited to the products granted by their user groups; the dashboard also hides three Analytics sections, and the Datasets, Versions, Tutorial and Configuration entries of the product sidebar, from them. They cannot create, update, or delete. HUMAN_EVALUATOR additionally may annotate AI metrics and submit human evaluations, on those products only; READER cannot. On an evaluation page, the dashboard also hides the evaluation metadata, the inference metrics and the Turn metrics tab of the Trace card from READER.
- **Product access**: A READER or HUMAN_EVALUATOR sees a product only when one of their user groups links it. A user group with the all-products setting grants every product of the organization, current and future. A user in no group sees no product. Records with no product, such as a global dataset, stay readable. Products outside the granted set answer like a record of another organization. In a user group, their `product_ids` list only the granted products. OWNER and MEMBER are not limited this way.
- **Organization scope**: "In own org" means the record (or its parent, such as a Product) has an organizationId equal to the signed-in user's organization. This keeps each client organization's data separate.
- **Parent scope**: For nested records (Version, Dataset, Evaluation, and so on) access is derived from the parent's organization. Example: a Version belongs to a Product, and the Product belongs to an Organization. An Evaluation is reached through session to version to product.
- **Global records**: Some records (Metric, Model, and a Dataset with no product) can be global, meaning not tied to one organization. Global records are readable by every role in addition to their own organization's records.
- **Hard delete**: Each entity table has two delete rows. **Soft delete** hides a record without removing it. **Delete** is a permanent removal, shown as allowed for Session (OWNER and MEMBER, within their own organization) and for User Group (OWNER only, within their own organization); for every other entity it is denied to all roles listed here.
