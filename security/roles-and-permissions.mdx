Galtea uses role-based access control. Every member of your organization has one role that decides what they can see and change. This page lists the four organization roles and the actions each one can perform. Actions cover the standard operations (Create, Read, Update, Delete) plus a soft delete (a logical delete that hides a record without removing it). Many permissions are conditional, shown as a short rule in the cell.

## Roles

- **OWNER**: Manages users and resources in their own organization. Can permanently delete only Sessions and User Groups; every other entity is soft delete at most. Only OWNER may create, edit, or delete a User Group, or change its users or products.
- **MEMBER**: Day-to-day access in the products their user groups grant: view and contribute, with limited edits. Creates a product only with a user group that grants all products. Can permanently delete only Sessions; every other entity is soft delete at most. Reads and edits every metric and reads every User Group of the organization. Cannot create, edit, or delete a User Group, or change its users or products. May link or unlink a group of the organization to a metric it may edit.
- **HUMAN_EVALUATOR**: Read-only on the products their user groups grant, plus may annotate AI metrics and submit human evaluations on them. Can also update their own account settings (name, consents and MFA method).
- **READER**: Read-only on the products their user groups grant. Cannot create, update, or delete anything except their own account settings (name, consents and MFA method).

<Note>
  Each cell shows what a role can do for that action. ✅ means allowed and ❌ means not allowed. A short rule (for example, "If product in own org") means the action is allowed only when that rule is true at the time, checked against your organization and the record's ownership.
</Note>

## Notes

- **Read-only roles**: READER and HUMAN_EVALUATOR read what a MEMBER reads, except that metrics are limited to the ones the products granted by their user groups use (all of them for a group linked to all products); the dashboard also hides the Coverage section of Analytics, and the Datasets, Versions, Tutorial and Configuration entries of the product sidebar, from them. They cannot create, update, or delete, except their own account settings (name, consents and MFA method). HUMAN_EVALUATOR additionally may annotate AI metrics and submit human evaluations, on those products only; READER cannot. The dashboard also hides from READER the evaluation metadata on an evaluation page, and the judge tokens and judge credits of every evaluation list, the inference metrics of the evaluation, session and version pages, the Inference section of Analytics, the latency and cost columns of the versions list, the monitor credits, and the metrics tab of the Trace card; the API returns those figures to READER as null, and refuses a READER's sort on them with 400. Span latency and the Analytics version summary (latency, tokens and average cost) stay visible.
- **Product access**: A MEMBER, READER or HUMAN_EVALUATOR reaches a product, and everything inside it, only when one of their user groups links it. A MEMBER may also create and edit inside those products. A user group with the all-products setting grants every product of the organization, current and future. A user in no group reaches no product. Only a user in a group with the all-products setting creates a product. When a user who cannot create a product reaches exactly one product, sign-in opens that product's Analytics instead of the product list. Records with no product, such as a global dataset, stay readable. When read by id, a product outside the granted set answers like a record of another organization. On the dataset list, naming an ungranted product answers 403, and naming another organization's product returns no dataset. In a user group, their `product_ids` list only the granted products. For a READER or HUMAN_EVALUATOR, metrics follow the products: unless one of their groups links all products, a metric is visible only when a granted product evaluates it, links it to a specification, or monitors it, and every revision of that metric is visible then. Any other metric answers like a metric of another organization. A MEMBER reads and edits every metric of the organization, but cannot delete a metric that has results in a product outside the granted set: the API answers 403. A metric's `specification_ids` list only the specifications of granted products, and a user group's `metric_ids` only the visible metrics. The analytics impact of excluding a metric revision, and the data metric optimization learns from, count only the granted products. An OWNER is not limited this way.
- **Organization scope**: "In own org" means the record (or its parent, such as a Product) has an organizationId equal to the signed-in user's organization. This keeps each client organization's data separate.
- **Parent scope**: For nested records (Version, Dataset, Evaluation, and so on) access is derived from the parent's organization. Example: a Version belongs to a Product, and the Product belongs to an Organization. An Evaluation is reached through session to version to product.
- **Global records**: Some records (Metric, Model, and a Dataset with no product) can be global, meaning not tied to one organization. Global records are readable by every role in addition to their own organization's records, except that a READER or HUMAN_EVALUATOR in no all-products group sees a global metric only when a granted product uses it.
- **Hard delete**: Each entity table has two delete rows. **Soft delete** hides a record without removing it. **Delete** is a permanent removal, shown as allowed for Session (OWNER within their own organization, MEMBER within the products its user groups grant) and for User Group (OWNER only, within their own organization); for every other entity it is denied to all roles listed here.
