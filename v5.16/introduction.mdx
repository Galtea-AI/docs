## How It Works

Galtea helps you evaluate AI products through a repeatable test-measure-iterate cycle:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register Product">Register a [Product](/v5.16/concepts/product) to represent your AI functionality.</Step>
  <Step title="Define Specifications">Define [Specifications](/v5.16/concepts/product/specification) — testable behavioral expectations for your product (capabilities, inabilities, policies).</Step>
  <Step title="Generate Metrics & Datasets">Galtea [generates metrics](/v5.16/concepts/metric/ai-generation) and [datasets](/v5.16/concepts/product/dataset) from your specifications, or you create them manually.</Step>
  <Step title="Create Version">Define a new [Version](/v5.16/concepts/product/version) of your product to track changes over time.</Step>
  <Step title="Run Evaluations">Run [Evaluations](/v5.16/concepts/product/version/session/evaluation) — `evaluations.run()` resolves specs, datasets, and metrics automatically.</Step>
  <Step title="Analyze & Iterate">Review results in the Analytics dashboard, then iterate with new versions to track improvements.</Step>
</Steps>
