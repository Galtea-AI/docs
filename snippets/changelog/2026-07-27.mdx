## Monitor Your Production Traffic Automatically

Testing tells you an AI product is ready to ship. **Monitors** tell you it is still behaving once real users arrive. A [Monitor](/concepts/product/monitor) is an always-on evaluation rule: point it at a [product](/concepts/product), pick the [metrics](/concepts/metric) that matter, and Galtea keeps scoring your live production sessions on its own. There is no run to trigger, no schedule to maintain, and no extra code once your product logs its sessions.

This is the difference between finding a quality regression in a weekly report and catching it as it happens. A Monitor watches the traffic your users actually send, so a drop in answer quality, a new failure pattern, or a prompt change that quietly hurt responses shows up on its own.

- **Set it once, watch continuously.** Create a Monitor from a product's **Monitors** tab (Production view). Each Monitor scores a sampled share of that product's production sessions — 10% by default — so you get a representative quality signal without paying to score every single conversation. Raise or lower the sampling rate as your traffic and budget change.
- **Metrics stay current on their own.** A Monitor binds a metric *family*, not a fixed version. When you revise a metric, the Monitor picks up the new revision automatically — you never re-edit the Monitor.
- **Spend stays under control.** An optional monthly credit cap protects each Monitor's spend, and your organization balance is always the hard limit. The Monitor's status tells you exactly why it is or is not scoring right now: `ACTIVE`, `PAUSED`, `CAPPED`, or `NO_CREDITS`.

Get started with [Monitor Production Responses](/sdk/tutorials/monitor-production-responses-to-user-queries), or point a Monitor at [real user traffic ingested over OpenTelemetry](/sdk/tutorials/monitor-real-user-traffic-with-opentelemetry).

### See the Score Trend Across the Whole Product

A Monitor's own page shows the results of that one Monitor. To see the trend across every production evaluation of a product, open its **Analytics** tab in **Production** view mode: an **evaluation scores over time** chart plots the average score per day. Switch between a **by metric** view (one line per metric family) and a **by specification** view (one line per [specification](/concepts/product/specification), built from the metrics linked to it). The chart follows the same filters as the rest of the page (version, test, metric, language, and time frame) and includes every matching production evaluation, including the ones you triggered by hand.

### Sessions Close Themselves, so Monitors Know When to Score

A Monitor scores a [session](/concepts/product/version/session) only once the conversation is over. So that Galtea knows when that is, a session now closes on its own after a period of inactivity, instead of staying open forever waiting for your integration to end it.

- **Configure it per product.** In the product's advanced settings you set the auto-close scope (`NONE`, `DEVELOPMENT`, `PRODUCTION`, or `ALL` — the default is `PRODUCTION`), the inactivity window in minutes (default 30), and what happens if a new turn arrives for an already-closed session: `REJECT` it with an error, or `IGNORE` it silently.
- **Close a conversation the moment it ends.** When you already know a conversation is finished, close it right away with the SDK's [`sessions.finish(session_id)`](/sdk/api/session/finish) so its Monitor scores it on the next scan instead of waiting out the inactivity window.
- **Know when production will not be scored.** If a product's auto-close scope excludes production, open production sessions never close on inactivity, so their Monitors score only the sessions you finish explicitly. The dashboard warns you when that is the case.

### Evaluation Scores Over Time Moved to the Production View

The scores-over-time chart announced in the 2026-07-21 release now appears only in the **Production** view of a product's Analytics tab. It plots one average score per day, which answers "is the live product drifting", so it belongs next to your Monitors rather than beside your test runs. Development still compares versions with the existing scores and performance charts. Details in [Monitor](/concepts/product/monitor).

## Manage Metrics and Tests Directly from a Specification

A [specification](/concepts/product/specification) is now the single place to manage its [metrics](/concepts/metric) and [tests](/concepts/product/test).

- **Link an existing metric or test.** From a specification's Hub tabs, the **Metrics** tab's create dropdown gains a **Link existing metric** action, and the **Tests** tab's create button becomes a dropdown with **Create new test** (opens the form already linked to the specification) and **Link existing test**.
- **Linking moves a test.** A test belongs to only one specification. If the test you pick is already linked elsewhere, a warning icon shows which specification it will move from.
- **Unlink instead of delete.** Inside a specification's Hub tabs, the bulk row action is **Unlink from specification** rather than delete, so removing a metric or test from a specification never deletes it.
- **See the link from the other side.** A metric's or test's own detail page now shows its linked specification(s) read-only, and the test creation form always states whether the new test will be linked, and to which specification.

## Comment on Inference Results

You can now leave free-form comments on an [inference result](/concepts/product/version/session/inference-result) to annotate a single turn during error analysis, without going through the metric-bound evaluation flow.

- Add and delete comments from the new **Comments** tab in the inference result detail panel. Comments support Markdown and show the author and timestamp.
- A turn card shows a comment icon (with a count when there is more than one) so you can spot annotated turns without opening them.
- The [sessions](/concepts/product/version/session) list has a new **Has comments** / **No comments** filter.

## Platform Improvements

- **Auto-close settings visible on product details:** The product [details](/concepts/product) page now shows auto-close scope, inactivity window, and closed-session inference policy read-only. Previously these were visible only on the edit page, behind Advanced Options.
