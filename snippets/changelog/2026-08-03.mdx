## Monitor Only the Production Traffic That Matters

A [Monitor](/concepts/product/monitor) scores a sampled share of your production [sessions](/concepts/product/version/session). The new **Content filter** section of the create and edit form tells it *which* sessions are worth scoring at all: refund and cancellation traffic for a support agent, everything except your internal test phrases for a medical assistant.

- **Include and exclude terms.** **Include terms** keeps only the sessions containing one of your terms. **Exclude terms** drops the sessions containing one of them, even if they matched an include term. Both are optional.
- **Write terms the simple way.** Separate terms with a comma, for example `refund, cancel, chargeback`. A session matches when any term appears anywhere in a turn, and matching is case-insensitive.
- **Choose which side of the turn to search.** Each group has its own **Match in** setting: the user's `Input`, your product's `Output`, or `Either`.
- **See the effect before you save.** A **Preview** panel tests the filter against your recent sessions and spends no credits: how many contain your terms, roughly how many would be evaluated at your current sampling percentage, and which ones match, so you can click into one and check.

Two limits to know. Sampling applies on top of the filter, so you get roughly your sampling percentage of the *matching* sessions, not of all traffic. And the filter only affects sessions that close after you save it, never past traffic. A Monitor's **Overview** tab states its filter in plain language.

Content filters work in the dashboard and on `POST /monitors` and `PATCH /monitors/{id}`. The Python SDK does not set them yet, so a Monitor created with [`monitors.create`](/sdk/api/monitor/create) has no filter until you add one in the dashboard.

## Label Your Comments and Find the Same Problem Across Sessions

Comments on an [inference result](/concepts/product/version/session/inference-result) can now carry labels, so error analysis produces something you can search instead of a pile of notes.

- **Label as you write.** The **Comments** tab of a turn has a **Labels for this comment** field. Labels are free text you invent as you go, for example `hallucination` or `wrong tone`, up to 10 per comment.
- **Reuse instead of retyping.** Every label already used on that [product](/concepts/product) appears above the field as a chip you can click, so the same wording lands on the same problem across many turns, by anyone on your team.
- **Filter sessions by label.** The [sessions](/concepts/product/version/session) list has a **Comments** filter offering **No comments**, **Has comments**, and one entry per label your product uses. Picking more than one widens the result.

On the API, the sessions endpoint replaces the `hasComments` parameter announced in the 2026-07-27 release with `commentLabels`, which takes `__none__`, `__any__`, or any label. The Python SDK does not cover comments yet.

## A Version Does Not Need a Connection Anymore

The [version](/concepts/product/version) form now starts with a **Link Connection** toggle, off by default, instead of asking for connection fields whether or not you have a live product to point at. Leave it off for a version that only records the [inferences](/concepts/product/version/session/inference-result) you send from your own code: the conversation target and connection fields stay hidden. Turn it on to have Galtea run conversations against your product, then pick the target (HTTP endpoint, phone, or WebRTC) and a connection, now mandatory, so no version is half-connected by accident. Turning the toggle off on an existing version and saving removes its connection.

See [Version](/concepts/product/version) for what each connection type does, or [Direct Inferences and Evaluations](/sdk/tutorials/direct-inferences-and-evaluations-from-platform) for the flow that needs a linked endpoint.

## Platform Improvements

- **Check which platform version you are running:** An **About** card at the end of the **Settings** page shows the deployed **Version**, **Commit**, and **Build date**, so an enterprise organization on its own infrastructure can compare it against the hosted platform. Visible to enterprise members and admins, and also available with `galtea build-info`.
- **Full session lifecycle documented:** The [Session](/concepts/product/version/session) page now covers which `Status` values mean a session is open, the two ways it closes ([`sessions.finish`](/sdk/api/session/finish) or the auto-close inactivity window), and what the closed-session policy does to a turn that arrives late.
- **Specification descriptions during onboarding:** The step for choosing which [specifications](/concepts/product/specification) to evaluate now shows a **Description** column, so you can tell similarly named specifications apart.
- **Detail tabs renamed:** The first tab on a version, session, test, test case, monitor, or organization page is now **Overview** instead of **Details**, matching the specification and user group pages. On a product it is now **Settings**.
- **The dashboard follows your system appearance:** With the theme set to **System**, the dashboard switches between light and dark the moment your operating system does, without a reload.
